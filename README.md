### Frequently asked questions

**How do I make square product photos?**

Choose the 1080×1080 White Pad preset to keep the whole product or Center Crop to trim the centre of the photo.

**Can it turn Greek filenames into Greeklish?**

Yes. Choose Greeklish SEO Slug in Batch Rename and check the preview before applying changes.

**Does it modify original photos?**

Image processing writes new files to NillyBatch_Output. Batch Rename acts on selected files and can undo the last rename.
